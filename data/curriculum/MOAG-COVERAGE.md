# Moag lesson coverage and learning support

Moag is the core course for this project. It already supplies English grammar explanations, bilingual vocabulary, contextual examples, reading patterns and exercises throughout its 25 main lessons. The companion work should make that material easier to study and use independently, then fill specific practice gaps. This map describes the improved local transcription reviewed on October 4, 2026; it does not replace its lessons.

The audit covers all 25 lessons, seven minilessons, two script chapters, six appendices, the glossary and the index. It inspected lesson organization and exercise sections throughout, with selected explanatory and contextual passages. It also checked the digital guide, writing tools, audio inventory and existing proofreading review. It did not listen to recordings, re-proofread every sentence, test learners or assign proficiency levels. The [structured audit](moag-audit.json) records the source-file hashes so later transcription changes can be identified.

## Audio follow up

A subsequent [recording audit](../audio/moag/AUDIO-AUDIT.md) decoded and checked all 25 tracks and used full-file automatic recognition to locate English section announcements. It recovered recorded repetition, slower/normal readings or dialogues, oral drills and selected spoken feedback. The original audit below remains the baseline: exact Malayalam alignment is still unverified, while the new cue map makes the existing material easier to investigate. The companion priority is now to verify and organize these recordings, then fill the remaining gaps.

## How to read the lesson map

The practical objectives below are editorial interpretations, not source learning outcomes or proficiency claims. Topic and practice summaries describe the inspected sections; suggested support and prerequisite links are recommendations. Each main lesson already has English explanation, examples, vocabulary, reading practice and exercises. Per-lesson feedback is modeled rather than a complete answer key. A lesson-named MP3 exists for every main lesson, but alignment is unverified. These shared facts are not repeated in every table cell.

Speaking means active oral response or repetition, not merely the presence of a printed dialogue. Writing exercises and reading-pattern practice do not establish handwriting mastery. A later or earlier form used as a glossed chunk is not automatically a curriculum defect.

## Gaps and existing editorial work

See the [gap backlog](MOAG-GAPS.md) for categories, priorities, evidence, dependencies and completion criteria. It separates learning support from source defects already tracked in the [proofreading review](../../docs/proofreading-review.md). Resolve or recheck those defects through the ongoing transcription work before using the affected rule in assessed companion practice.

Future audience routes should distinguish age, existing spoken Malayalam, script literacy, purpose and activity preference. They are extensions of this core map, not evidence that Moag lacks grammar or that all heritage learners need the same course. The audit creates no new learner materials and changes no transcription files.
